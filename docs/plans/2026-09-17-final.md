_Plan of 2026-09-17. Controller: Fable 5.1. Implementers and reviewers: Opus subagents._

The exhibit is complete and live; this run adds what a museum showpiece still lacks (launch

## Global Constraints

These bind every task. The reviewer checks them.

1. **No em dashes anywhere.** Not in code, comments, copy, HTML, JSON, docs or commit messages
   (U+2014 and the `&mdash;` entity are both forbidden). Use a comma, colon, full stop or a spaced
   hyphen. `grep -rnP "\x{2014}" web/src web/index.html web/public/audio tools docs *.md` must print nothing.
2. **Both versions get every feature.** The vitrine (default) and the manuscript (`?theme=manuscript`)
   are the same app; a feature is built once and differs only through the existing `[data-theme]`
   CSS tokens and `setTheme` hooks. Never change vitrine styling to suit the manuscript.
3. **Copy in the exhibit's voice**: a museum label. Lowercase button labels like the existing ones
   ("back to epoch", "taken apart"), no title case, no exclamation marks, no emoji, nothing that
   reads as an app. Every claim exact.
4. **Time conventions untouched**: Julian Day inside, proleptic Julian for BC, astronomical years.
   Never JS `Date` for BC dates. `Date` may read the present (AD) and is converted with
   `civilToJdn` from `web/src/astro/jd.ts`.
5. **Motion only where the machine moves; nothing decorative animates.** A label may change text; it
   may not bounce, fade in, or slide.
6. **Do not touch** `data/gears.json`, `blender/`, `python/`, the GLB or the textures. The web
   layer only (plus `tools/` scripts that write into `web/public/`).
7. **Verification before every commit**: `cd web && npx tsc --noEmit && npx vitest run && npx vite build`.
   All three must pass with pristine output. Python tests are unaffected and need not run.
8. **Do not run the dev server and do not use any browser tool.** A dev server on port 5177 is
   owned by the controller, who verifies in the browser after each task. Do not start Blender.
9. **One commit per task** (more if a task has natural halves), message in the repository's own
   style: one sentence in plain English describing what changed and why, no conventional-commit
   prefix, no bullet lists, ending with the line `Co-Authored-By: Claude Fable 5.1 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.
   Do not push. Work on the branch `v1`, which is checked out.
10. **Keep files focused.** `web/src/main.ts` (610 lines) and `web/src/scene/viewer.ts` (1100 lines)
    are already large; add to them only what belongs there and prefer a small new module for
    anything self-contained (a service worker, an icon generator, a snapshot helper).
11. **Existing behaviour is the spec.** Do not refactor, rename or restyle beyond the task.

### The model

Two alphabetic sequences, one per plate, four columns of one season each, running clockwise round
the dial: PP1 (the plate above the dial) has col. i Capricorn to Pisces with letters Α to Θ and
col. ii Aries to Gemini with Ι to Σ; PP2 (below) has col. iii Libra to Sagittarius with Α to Λ and
col. iv Cancer to Virgo with Μ to Ω. A letter is engraved on the zodiac scale immediately clockwise
of a graduation mark; graduation 1 is the sign boundary, so the degree into the sign (0-based)
is the graduation number minus one.

Positions, as the digest gives them (sign, letter, graduation, degree into sign, status):

- Read on the bronze of Fragment C (status `scale`): Virgo Ψ 19 (18°), Virgo Ω 21 (20°),
  Libra Α 1 (0°), Libra Β 11 (10°), Libra Γ 14 (13°), Libra Δ 16 (15°), Scorpio Ε 1 (0°),
  Scorpio Ζ 4 (3°), Scorpio Η 17 (16°), Scorpio Θ 22 (21°), Sagittarius Ι 1 (0°),
  Sagittarius Κ 3 (2°), Sagittarius Λ 7 (6°).
- Attested by the numeral written after the parapegma line (status `numeral`): Aries Λ 21 (20°;
  the paper's Greek reads ΚΑ, its English says 24; use 21 and say so in a comment),
  Taurus Μ 1 (0°), Taurus Ν 11 (10°), Taurus Ξ 17 (16°), Taurus Ο 25 (24°), Gemini Σ 10 (9°).
- Restored by the editors (status `restored`): Aries Ι 1 (0°), Gemini Π 1 (0°), and the other
  sign-entry letters that the reconstruction places at graduation 1: Capricorn Α, Cancer Μ, Leo Π.
- Everything else is lost (status `lost`, no position): PP1 col. i Β to Θ (one of them carries the
  numeral 11, probably in Pisces, but the letter is uncertain, so treat it as lost), PP1 col. ii Κ
  and Ρ, PP2 col. iv Ν, Ξ, Ο (events known, degrees lost) and Ρ to Χ.

Events (English, as the digest translates them; keep this wording): Capricorn Α "Capricorn begins
to rise" and the winter solstice at 1; Aries Ι "Aries begins to rise", the vernal equinox at 1;
Aries Κ "the Pleiades set in the evening" (tentative, lost); Aries Λ "the Hyades set in the
evening"; Taurus Μ "Taurus begins to rise"; Taurus Ν "Lyra rises in the evening"; Taurus Ξ "the
Pleiades rise in the morning"; Taurus Ο "the Hyades rise in the morning"; Gemini Π "Gemini begin to
rise"; Gemini Ρ "Aquila rises in the evening" (lost); Gemini Σ "Arcturus sets in the morning";
Libra Α "the Claws begin to rise", the autumnal equinox at 1; Libra Β "the Haedi rise in the
evening" (restored name); Libra Γ "the Pleiades rise in the evening" (restored name); Libra Δ
"Corona rises in the morning" (restored name); Scorpio Ε "Scorpio begins to rise"; Scorpio Ζ, Η,
Θ and Sagittarius Κ, Λ: text lost, position known; Sagittarius Ι "Sagittarius begins to rise"
(inferred from the letter at the boundary); Cancer Μ "Cancer begins to rise", the summer solstice
at 1; Cancer Ν "Orion rises in the morning"; Cancer Ξ "Sirius rises in the morning"; Cancer Ο
"Aquila sets in the morning"; Leo Π "Leo begins to rise"; Virgo Ψ "Capella rises in the evening"
(restored); Virgo Ω "Arcturus rises in the morning" (restored).
